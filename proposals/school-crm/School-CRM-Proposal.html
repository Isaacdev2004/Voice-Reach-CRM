1. Executive Summary

[School Name] needs a centralized system to manage family inquiries, admissions follow-up, communication, and enrollment pipeline — without juggling spreadsheets, email threads, and disconnected tools.

We recommend a custom school CRM built on our existing ARI platform (proven CRM + communications engine), configured for education instead of real estate.

This approach delivers:

  • Lower cost than enterprise school suites (often $1,000+/month)
  • Features tailored to this school's workflow
  • Dedicated account management with technical support behind the scenes
  • Room to grow (parent portal, forms, integrations) in later phases

Build vs. Buy:

  • Buy existing if they need full school ERP (grades, attendance, billing, cafeteria, etc.)
  • Custom build (recommended) if primary need is admissions CRM + parent communication + staff workflow

2. Scope of Work — Phase 1 (Recommended)

Included in Phase 1

A. Core CRM (School-Configured)

  • Family & contact records (parents/guardians, students, inquiry source)
  • Custom pipeline: Inquiry → Contacted → Tour Scheduled → Applied → Accepted → Enrolled → Lost
  • Notes, tags, and activity history per family
  • Staff roles: Admin, Admissions, Read-only

B. Communication

  • Email campaigns (newsletters, reminders, event invites)
  • SMS to parents (opt-in / consent tracking)
  • Message templates (tour reminder, application deadline, welcome packet)

C. Operations

  • Tasks & follow-ups (call back, send packet, schedule tour)
  • Calendar (tours, open houses, meetings)
  • Basic analytics (inquiries by month, conversion by stage, overdue tasks)

D. Data & Launch

  • Import existing contacts / spreadsheet
  • School branding (logo, colors, name)
  • Admin training session (1 × 60 minutes)
  • 30-day post-launch bug-fix window

E. Removed / Hidden (Real Estate–Specific)

  • Ringless voicemail, mortgage tools, property finder, Dotloop, RE pipeline labels

Phase 2 (Optional — Quoted Separately)

  • Online inquiry & application forms
  • Parent portal (view status, upload documents)
  • Event registration (open house RSVP)
  • Tuition / payment reminders
  • Google Classroom / SIS integration
  • AI assistant for staff

Out of Scope (Unless Added)

  • Full gradebook / attendance / state reporting
  • Payroll, accounting, cafeteria
  • Native mobile apps (web works on phone)
