ARI — Agent Experience

What agents do vs. what the platform handles
Agents sign up, we set them up, they run follow-up from one dashboard — no phone carriers, no developer setup.

Product: ARI (myari.io)

Date: September 22, 2026

Prepared by: Kikzeny Cartagena LLC

Version: Founding 100 · Launch

Short answer: Agents do not purchase their own phone numbers or configure Twilio, Vercel, Supabase, or webhooks. Messaging runs on the ARI platform. Agents create an account, get white-glove onboarding, and use the CRM to manage leads and campaigns.

Do agents need their own phone numbers for SMS?

No — not at launch. SMS is sent through ARI's platform Twilio account (configured once in production). Agents message from the shared ARI sending number. They do not open a Twilio account or buy a number separately.

Future option (Team / brokerage tier): dedicated local numbers per agent or office — optional add-on, not required at sign-up.

✓ What agents do in the app

  • Import leads (CSV or manual)
  • Build follow-up campaigns (SMS, email, ringless VM)
  • Record or generate voicemail in Voice Scripts
  • Launch campaigns when ready
  • Work dashboard — who to call next, replies, tasks
  • Optional: Google Calendar, Dotloop

✗ What agents never do

  • Buy Twilio numbers or SMS accounts
  • Configure Vercel, Supabase, or servers
  • Set up webhooks or API keys
  • Manage Slybroadcast, Resend, or ElevenLabs
  • Handle TCPA/DNC infrastructure manually

Platform backend (already configured — not agent-facing)

  • Production hosting & domain (myari.io)
  • Database & file storage (Supabase)
  • Twilio SMS + STOP/HELP compliance webhooks
  • Slybroadcast ringless voicemail delivery
  • Resend transactional email
  • ElevenLabs AI voice generation
  • Stripe subscriptions · Clerk authentication
  • Consent tracking, DNC scrubbing, quiet hours

Compliance note

ARI includes consent tracking, opt-out (STOP), DNC scrubbing, and quiet hours. Agents must only message contacts who have opted in. Platform handles delivery infrastructure; agents control who gets enrolled in campaigns.
